MAKE ROOM FOR YOUR THOUGHTS
A little conversation. A lasting thought.
  1. Say it out loud
    Open Talk and start speaking. No title or category is required. In connected mode, allow microphone access; headphones work best.
  2. Follow your train of thought
    Pause to get a reply. Speak again to interrupt. Each live session lasts up to two minutes.
  3. Take a different direction
    Open Library to review records, original voice notes, and documents. Review notes collects the current conversation into a record; automatic topic filing is not active yet.
Your workspace stays local. Connected mode uses real models; Demo mode is explicitly labeled and uses typed examples and fixed replies.
